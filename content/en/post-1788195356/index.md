# How Much Does Notion AI Cost? The Full Price Breakdown (2026)

You sit down Monday morning, coffee in hand, and need to pull together a project brief, summarize last week's meeting notes, and draft a client proposal — all inside Notion. You type a quick prompt, and the AI responds. Clean. Fast. Exactly what you paid for — or so you'd think. Except when you check your billing page, you realize you're on Plus and that prompt just cost you nothing because it was a limited trial slice… and the real question is sitting in your head: *how much does Notion AI actually cost, and am I paying the right amount?*

If that sounds familiar, you're not alone. Notion overhauled its entire AI pricing model in May 2025, and the change left a lot of users guessing. Here's what you need to know — the real numbers, the traps, and how to pick the right plan without overspending.

---

## What Changed: The Add-On Is Dead (Long Live the Bundle)

Before May 13, 2025, you could add Notion AI as a standalone supplement to almost any plan — roughly $8 to $10 per user per month. Simple. Choose your base plan, bolt on AI, done.

That option no longer exists for new users. Notion absorbed every AI feature — Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Ask Notion, Enterprise Search — exclusively into the **Business plan**. If you were already paying for the add-on, you were automatically migrated to Business at the same price you were paying, starting December 2025. But there's a catch: legacy subscribers can't re-subscribe if they cancel. Once you leave, that door closes.

## Every Notion Plan That Touches AI (and What You Actually Get)

Notion's current lineup has four tiers. Here's how AI fits into each one:

## The Hidden Cost: Custom Agents and Credits

Here's where budgets get tricky. Notion's **Custom Agents** — autonomous AI agents that handle tasks without constant supervision — run on a credit system separate from the base subscription.

They cost **$10 for every 1,000 credits per month**, and credits don't roll over. If you buy 3,000 credits and only burn 1,500, the remainder vanishes at month's end.

For a small team running agents daily, that can add $30 to $50 per user on top of the Business plan fee. If your team of five leans heavily on autonomous agents, you're looking at $100 to $150 per month — not the $100 baseline you might budget.

## Real-World Scenarios: What Teams Actually Pay

Let's make this concrete with three common setups:

**1. Freelancer on Plus ($10/month)**
You get basic AI — chat, document drafting, database autofill. That's enough for solo work if you're disciplined about prompts. But you won't have Notion Agent, meeting summaries, or Enterprise Search. Cost: $120/year. AI included, capped.

**2. Startup team of 5 on Business ($20/user/month, annual)**
Full AI suite for everyone. Meeting notes auto-generated after every Zoom call, agents pulling tickets from your database, search across your entire workspace. Cost: **$100/month, $1,200/year**. No hidden fees unless you activate Custom Agents.

**3. Team of 5 + heavy Custom Agent use (3,000 credits/user/month)**
Business base plus agent credits: $100 + $50 = **$150/month, $1,800/year**. That's the ceiling for a mid-sized team pushing AI hard.

## Is It Worth Upgrading from Plus to Business Just for AI?

Short answer: if you use Notion daily for team collaboration, yes. The math is straightforward — Business is $10 more per user per month than Plus, and it delivers Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Ask Notion, and Enterprise Search. If those features save you even one hour per week across the team, the ROI is there.

But if you're a solo user who mainly drafts documents and wants an occasional AI summary, Plus with its limited trial mode might be sufficient. The full Business price only makes sense when multiple people are actively benefiting from AI features simultaneously.

## Notion AI vs ChatGPT Plus — A Fair Comparison

This question comes up constantly: *should I pay for ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) and keep Notion Plus, or just go all-in on Notion Business?*

ChatGPT Plus gives you a general-purpose model that can write anything, but it lives outside your workspace. Notion AI lives inside your pages, databases, and meeting transcripts — it knows your context, your project structure, your team's notes. The $20/month for Notion Business buys you that contextual awareness, plus meeting transcription and autonomous agents. ChatGPT Plus buys you a smarter chatbot.

They're complementary, not interchangeable. Many teams run both — Notion Business for workspace AI and ChatGPT Plus for everything else. If budget forces a choice, go with whichever tool sits at the center of your daily workflow.

## Alternatives Worth a Look

Notion AI isn't the only game in town for workspace-integrated AI:

- **Obsidian + Copilot plugin** — cheaper per-seat for personal knowledge management, but no meeting notes or team features.
- **ClickUp Brain** — bundled into its own project management tool; worth comparing if you're already using ClickUp.
- **Coda AI** — similar concept to Notion, different pricing structure; good for teams that need logic-heavy automations.

Each trades something. Notion's advantage is the breadth of AI features packed into one plan. The disadvantage is that you pay for all of them even if you only need two.
