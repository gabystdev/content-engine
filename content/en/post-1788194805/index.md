# Which AI Writing Tool Is Best? The Answer Depends on What You Actually Write

## FAQ

**Is content written by AI detectable?**

Yes. Turnitin, GPTZero, and EyeSift detect unmodified AI-generated content with over 90% accuracy. Turnitin has analyzed 200 million papers through its AI indicator since launching in 2023. If you publish AI-generated text, edit it substantially and attribute it where required. The tools are only getting better at catching raw output.

**Do AI marketing tools actually move the needle?**

Aggregate benchmarks from Siege Media show measurable gains: 36% higher conversion rates on landing pages, 38% higher CTR in ads, and 32% lower CPC. Those numbers come from teams using AI as part of a structured workflow, not as a write-and-publish shortcut. The tools amplify a process; they don't replace one.

**Should I use an LLM or a specialized tool?**

Use an LLM (Claude or ChatGPT) for drafting — it's what they're best at. Use a specialized tool (Positive Surfer, Jasper, Grammarly) for what happens after: optimization, consistency, and editing. The market bifurcation in 2025–2026 makes this split the most efficient workflow. LLMs write. Specialized tools govern, optimize, and verify. Trying to do both with one tool means compromises on both sides.

**What's the cheapest way to test these tools?**

Claude and ChatGPT both offer free tiers with limited access. Rytr gives 10,000 characters/month free with no card. Positive Surfer's Discovery plan at $49/month is the lowest-cost entry into SEO-focused writing. Avoid annual commitments until you've tested at least two tools against your actual writing tasks — what works for blog posts may flop for product pages, and vice versa.
